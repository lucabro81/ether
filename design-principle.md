# Design principle

## Physical reality vs. tracked mechanic
Not every physical consequence needs a mechanical rule. Some effects are real within the fiction of the system but only tracked when they become dramatically relevant. The thermal drop from a single cast is always tracked — it's immediate and tactical. The cumulative environmental cooling from repeated casting in the same area is real, but made explicit only at GM discretion.
The test: does this need a rule, or does it need a note?

## Rule layers
The system supports (at least) two levels of engagement with physical consequences:

Base layer: thermal effects exist within the fiction, narrated and applied at GM discretion.
Survivor layer: thermal drop and heat release are tracked numerically with no narrative softening.

This applies to any mechanic with a physical basis that has both immediate tactical relevance and longer-term environmental consequences.
