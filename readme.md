# Ether — A Physics-Based Magic System

A classless tabletop/digital RPG magic system grounded in thermodynamics rather than mysticism. Designed with digital implementation as the primary target (MUD, text adventure, companion app), but functional for tabletop play.

## Premise

Magic is not a resource you spend. It is a physical phenomenon you interact with.
The world is permeated by an omnipresent background energy field — the Ether. 

In its natural state it is chaotic, incoherent noise: no direction, no structure, no intention.

In rare individuals, a biological anomaly allows them to draw from this field directly — extracting energy from the ambient noise and concentrating it into something workable. The mage is a channel, not a source. 

What they collimate is not the effect but the raw material: incoherent noise drawn into something directed enough to be used.

What happens next is a separate act. Extracted energy can be released with minimal shaping, a discharge that follows entropy's natural downward slope. Or it can be imposed into a structure — something ordered that actively resists its own dissolution. The first costs little beyond the extraction itself. The second costs proportionally to the order being maintained.

Energy is conserved. Every extraction depletes the surrounding field, manifesting as a temperature drop in the area. The less refined the channel, the larger the volume of environment required to supply the same amount of usable energy — and the wider the drop. The drop is not flavor. It is a tactical variable that affects everyone present.
