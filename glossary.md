# Glossary

### CHANNEL
A biological structure present in latent form across the population, distinct from the nervous system. Below a natural activation threshold it is completely inert; no interaction with the field occurs, no effect is produced, no manifestation is detectable. Above that threshold the channel begins to interact with the ether field spontaneously and involuntarily, which without training can be dangerous or destabilizing for the individual.

The nervous system serves as the interface through which conscious control over the channel is exercised. Training is the process of learning to use that interface deliberately and reliably. The role of a master is not to grant ability but to recognize spontaneous manifestation and train the individual to operate the channel consciously and safely.

Because the channel and the nervous system are distinct structures, their failure modes differ. Physical stress degrades the quality of the interface progressively. Acute mental stress — panic, shock — can saturate the interface entirely, collapsing the ability to impose structured effects while leaving the channel active. A saturated interface does not silence the channel; it removes conscious direction from it. Involuntary discharges under acute stress are the channel responding to an overwhelmed nervous system, not a failure of the channel itself.

Development toward the individual's natural ceiling requires training. That ceiling is determined by factors external to this system, physical and cognitive attributes defined by the host system. A channel trained beyond its natural capacity produces diminishing returns and increasing risk.

See also: [Channel Quality](#channel-quality), [Etheric System](#etheric-system), [Instability](#instability).

### CHANNEL QUALITY
A measure of the channel's efficiency and stability. Operates on a continuum; there is no binary threshold between "mage" and "non-mage", only a practical floor below which the channel cannot perform useful work regardless of training.
Determines three things:

- **Extraction efficiency:** how much usable energy is yielded per unit of field depleted. A poor channel wastes more, drawing from a larger volume to achieve the same result.
- **Thermal drop radius:** direct consequence of extraction efficiency. The wider the draw volume, the wider the temperature drop.
- **EP accumulation rate:** a less efficient channel accumulates pressure faster for the same output.

Channel quality has a natural ceiling determined by anatomy; training can develop the channel toward that ceiling but not beyond it. Whether that ceiling can be artificially exceeded, and at what cost, is addressed under the [Rogue Path](#rogue-path).
Degradation is possible. Sustained operation beyond safe EP thresholds, backlash from volatile components, and extraction from living sources all carry risk of permanent channel damage. Damage lowers the effective ceiling.

See also: [Channel](#channel), [Extraction](#extraction), [Thermal Drop](#thermal-drop).

### COHERENCE ROLL
The check that determines whether the structure imposed during structuring holds as intended. A structured effect requires that the mage maintain coherent control over the ordered form; the more complex the structure, the higher the EP pressure, and the lower the channel quality, the greater the risk of partial or total coherence failure.
Failure does not necessarily mean nothing happens. It means the structure collapses in an uncontrolled way; energy is released, but not as intended. The consequences scale with how much energy was involved.
Coherence Roll is influenced by: Complexity of the effect, current EP level, Channel Quality, and Mastery.

See also: [Complexity](#complexity), [Instability](#instability), [Mastery](#mastery), [Structuring](#structuring).

### COLLIMATION
The mechanism by which extraction occurs. Incoherent, dispersed field energy is brought into coherence, given direction and concentration without yet being shaped into an effect. The mage's biological channel determines collimation quality: a refined channel collimates efficiently from a small volume; a poor channel must draw from a larger volume to achieve the same energy yield, producing a wider thermal drop.
Collimation is extraction-scoped only. It describes how energy is gathered, not what is done with it afterward.

See also: [Channel Quality](#channel-quality), [Extraction](#extraction), [Thermal Drop](#thermal-drop).

### COMPLEXITY
A property of the structure being imposed, not an axis competing for the energy budget directly. Describes how intricate the ordered form is, how many independent constraints must be maintained simultaneously. Acts as a tax on the available budget before it is distributed across the other axes.
Complexity is not chosen freely; it is determined by the nature of the effect.

See also: [Coherence Roll](#coherence-roll), [Mastery](#mastery), [Structuring](#structuring).

### COMPONENT
A physical material that contains ether within its structure and can be used as an ether source during extraction, reducing or eliminating reliance on the ambient field. Components are depleted through use. Which materials qualify, and their properties, are determined empirically; no unified theory explains why a given material holds ether or in what quantity.

See also: [Component Quality](#component-quality), [Ether Source](#ether-source), [Thermal Drop](#thermal-drop).

### COMPONENT QUALITY
Components contain ether within their physical structure through mechanisms not fully understood, known empirically, not theoretically. Characterized by two independent properties:

- **Concentration:** total ether stored. Additive when components are combined.
- **Release rate:** how quickly ether becomes available during extraction. Low rate means controlled, predictable extraction. High rate means powerful but potentially uncontrolled; excess energy hitting the channel causes backlash. When components are combined, release rate is the weighted average of the individual components.

These two properties are independent. A component can be highly concentrated and stable or highly concentrated and volatile. Combinations allow partial control over the tradeoff.

See also: [Component](#component), [Instability](#instability).

### CONSUMPTION
One of two distinct degradation processes associated with the [Rogue Path](#rogue-path). Consumption is progressive structural loss of conscious control over the channel. The channel itself remains intact; what degrades is the architecture of deliberate direction built through training.

The endpoint is worse than the pre-training state. A young untrained mage produces involuntary discharges from a structurally intact system with limited capacity. A mage in terminal consumption produces involuntary discharges from a system that has spent years learning to move large amounts of energy, with no direction over it. The more capable the mage, the more catastrophic the loss.

Past the point of no return, the ceiling of the channel is permanently lowered and the safe operating range narrows. Untreated, degradation continues; the condition does not plateau. Management through substances, practices, or both can stabilize the parameters, but without continued maintenance the condition reasserts itself.

Consumption is not a choice. It accumulates as a consequence of sustained overextension of the channel.

See also: [Channel](#channel), [Instability](#instability), [Poisoning](#poisoning), [Rogue Path](#rogue-path).

### DAMAGE TYPE
The physical nature of a structured effect's output. Not a mechanical choice made by the mage; it is determined by how the extracted energy is structured and released. Ether defines the base types that emerge naturally from the physics of the system. Modifiers, resistances, and interactions with specific targets belong to the host system.
Base types:

- **Fire:** thermal energy released outward. Consequence of high-magnitude rapid energy discharge.
- **Cold:** forced extraction from a target volume. Not a separate energy type; it is the thermal drop weaponized and directed.
- **Impact:** kinetic energy distributed over a surface. Force applied broadly.
- **Piercing:** kinetic energy concentrated on a minimal contact point. Force applied narrowly.
- **Lightning:** electromagnetic discharge. Rapid, poorly controlled energy release along paths of least resistance.

*Note: cold is mechanically distinct from the other types; it does not add energy to a target but removes it. This may have implications for resistances and interactions defined by the host system.*

See also: [Magnitude](#magnitude), [Structuring](#structuring), [Thermal Drop](#thermal-drop).

### DISTANCE
How far from the caster the structured effect is projected or applied. Projecting structure across space has an energy cost proportional to that distance. Competes with Magnitude and Duration for the available budget.

See also: [Duration](#duration), [Magnitude](#magnitude), [Structuring](#structuring).

### DURATION
How long a structured effect persists before collapsing. Maintaining structure over time requires ongoing energy; Duration is not a one-time cost but a continuous draw against the budget.
Duration cost scales with the volume over which the structure must be maintained. A persistent effect spread over an area costs proportionally more than the same effect on a single point. Spread is not a separate axis; it is a multiplier on Duration cost. For instantaneous effects, spread carries no additional energy cost beyond diluting Magnitude over the affected area.

See also: [Distance](#distance), [Magnitude](#magnitude), [Structuring](#structuring).

### ENERGY BALANCE
Energy removed from the local field equals energy made available to the mage. Conservation holds: nothing is created. The thermal drop is the physical trace of this transaction.

See also: [Entropic Balance](#entropic-balance), [Ether](#ether), [Thermal Drop](#thermal-drop).

### ENTROPIC BALANCE
Extraction concentrates dispersed energy, locally reducing the field's entropy. This is thermodynamically permitted because any ordered structure the mage imposes will eventually dissolve, releasing that entropy back. The books balance over time, not in the immediate environment.
Mechanically relevant only as a constraint on structured effects; they are inherently temporary.

See also: [Energy Balance](#energy-balance), [Structuring](#structuring).

### ETHER
A pervasive energy field present throughout all matter and space. In its natural state it exists at maximum entropy: energy is everywhere but distributed with no coherence, direction, or structure. Not a finite global resource, but local density is finite and depletable in the short term.

*Note: cultural or mystical interpretations of the field have no mechanical effect.*

See also: [Energy Balance](#energy-balance), [Entropic Balance](#entropic-balance), [Ether Source](#ether-source).

### ETHER SOURCE
The origin point from which raw energy is drawn during extraction. Three types:

- **Ambient field:** energy drawn from the local environment including all matter in proximity, potentially including the caster. Produces full thermal drop. Default when no alternative is available.
- **Components:** materials identified through magical theory or empirical practice as concentrated ether sources. Usable individually or in combination. Reduce ambient extraction proportionally to their quality, reducing thermal drop accordingly. A perfect component would eliminate ambient extraction entirely; in practice, quality determines how close to that ceiling a component gets. The component depletes instead. Channel strain is unaffected.
- **Living sources:** addressed separately under [Rogue Path](#rogue-path).

*Note: component quality is a primitive to be defined. The caster's own body as ambient source is addressed under Ether Pressure.*

See also: [Component](#component), [Extraction](#extraction), [Rogue Path](#rogue-path), [Thermal Drop](#thermal-drop).

### ETHERIC SYSTEM
See [Channel](#channel). Academic and formal term for the same biological structure. Used in institutional and scholarly contexts; "channel" is the common usage.

### EXTRACTION
The act of drawing energy from the local Ether field and concentrating it into a usable form. Always produces a thermal drop. The mage does not generate energy; they displace it from the field into a controlled state. Extraction is a prerequisite for any magical effect; there is no structuring without prior extraction.

See also: [Collimation](#collimation), [Ether Source](#ether-source), [Structuring](#structuring), [Thermal Drop](#thermal-drop).

### INSTABILITY
A condition of the channel, not of a single cast. Accumulates when the channel is operated under stress: high EP, coherence failures, backlash from volatile components. Represents progressive degradation of the channel's ability to impose and maintain ordered structure reliably. Distinct from the corruption processes described under [Consumption](#consumption) and [Poisoning](#poisoning); Instability is mechanical wear, not contamination or structural loss of control.
Instability manifests in two ways:

- **Short term:** increases the probability and severity of coherence failures. An unstable channel wastes more of the energy budget, produces unpredictable effects, and accumulates EP faster.
- **Long term:** sustained instability risks permanent channel degradation, lowering the natural ceiling, not just the current performance.

Instability is not binary. It sits on a continuum and can be reduced through rest, recovery, and conditions defined by the host system.

See also: [Channel Quality](#channel-quality), [Coherence Roll](#coherence-roll), [Consumption](#consumption), [Poisoning](#poisoning).

### MAGNITUDE
The amount of effective energy delivered by a structured effect. Represents the raw "power" of the output: heat, force, light, or any other physical manifestation. Competes directly with Duration and Distance for the available energy budget.

See also: [Distance](#distance), [Duration](#duration), [Structuring](#structuring).

### MASTERY
The trained efficiency with which a mage imposes and maintains structure. Not a measure of raw power; that is determined by extraction and the energy budget. Mastery determines how much of that budget survives the complexity tax.
A high Mastery mage imposing a complex structure wastes less energy maintaining form; the same budget yields better results across the axes. Mastery also reduces the probability and severity of coherence failure under pressure.
Mastery is developed through training and experience. It has a natural ceiling constrained by factors supplied by the host system.

See also: [Coherence Roll](#coherence-roll), [Complexity](#complexity), [Structuring](#structuring).

### POISONING
One of two distinct degradation processes associated with the [Rogue Path](#rogue-path). Poisoning is progressive contamination of the channel through extraction from living sources. The channel does not deteriorate; it changes. The mage's capacity may increase in raw magnitude while precision and self-assessment degrade.

The defining characteristic of poisoning is that the mage cannot accurately perceive it. The contamination distorts the ability to evaluate one's own condition. The mage believes they are in control; the degree to which that belief is accurate decreases over time.

An early and diagnostically significant symptom: the mage begins to prefer dirty ether. This craving precedes visible behavioral changes and presents as a practical preference rather than a personality shift. At advanced stages, deliberate negligence about component supply is common; the absence of clean alternatives stops feeling like a problem.

Past the point of no return, the contamination cannot be fully eliminated. The craving is suppressed, not removed. Perceptual distortion has a permanent baseline. Resistance to further contamination is compromised. Untreated, degradation continues. Management through substances, practices, or both can temporarily restore parameters close to normal, but without continued maintenance the condition reasserts itself.

See also: [Consumption](#consumption), [Ether Source](#ether-source), [Instability](#instability), [Rogue Path](#rogue-path).

### ROGUE PATH
The trajectory of degradation that opens when a mage extracts from living sources or sustains chronic overextension of the channel. Not a class or deliberate choice; a set of consequences that accumulates through use. Available to anyone with sufficient awareness of their own etheric system, trained mage or otherwise.

Introduces two qualitatively distinct processes: [Consumption](#consumption) and [Poisoning](#poisoning). Both can run simultaneously in the same individual.

The symptom progression is partially known. A public list of observable symptoms exists in the world as accumulated lore; the exact threshold at which each symptom manifests varies by individual and is not externally legible. From the midpoint of the progression onward, each stage carries a growing probability of being the point of no return. The mage knows the window exists; they do not know if they are still inside it.

Past the point of no return the damage is permanent but not static. Untreated, both processes continue to degrade. Management is possible; full recovery belongs to the host system.

*The corruption curve, symptom divergence between processes, and calibration of the point of no return require dedicated mechanic design.*

See also: [Consumption](#consumption), [Ether Source](#ether-source), [Instability](#instability), [Poisoning](#poisoning).

### STRUCTURING
The act of imposing form on extracted energy. A separate step from extraction; collimation ends here, structuring begins. Two modes:

- **Discharge:** extracted energy is released with minimal shaping, following the natural entropic gradient. Cost is low, essentially the extraction itself. The effect is immediate, uncontrolled, and non-persistent. The energy returns to the field.
- **Structured effect:** extracted energy is held in an ordered state that actively resists dissolution. Cost is proportional to the degree of order being maintained, not a one-time payment but an ongoing one for as long as the structure persists. When the mage stops sustaining it, the structure collapses and entropy is released.

The difference between the two modes is not magnitude; a discharge can carry as much energy as a structured effect. The difference is control over form and persistence. Discharge is a hammer. Structured effect is a scalpel you have to keep holding.

*Note: "order" here is an informal placeholder. The axes that define what dimensions of order exist, and therefore what structuring actually costs, are defined separately.*

See also: [Coherence Roll](#coherence-roll), [Complexity](#complexity), [Extraction](#extraction), [Mastery](#mastery).

### THERMAL DROP
Direct consequence of extraction and collimation. When energy is removed from a local volume of the field, that volume's energy density decreases, manifesting as a measurable temperature drop. Proportional to the amount extracted, inversely proportional to channel quality. If the extracted energy is released at a different location, the extraction point remains cold; no local reequilibration occurs. Repeated extraction in the same area produces cumulative cooling; this is physically real but not mechanically tracked unless dramatically relevant.

See also: [Collimation](#collimation), [Energy Balance](#energy-balance), [Extraction](#extraction).
